Shop conversation — full transcript

Customer: Excuse me. What's the price of this cheese, please?

Shopkeeper: It's eight riyals for one packet.

Customer: OK, thank you. Can I have one kilo of rice, and a bottle of water, too?

Shopkeeper: Yes, of course. That's fifteen riyals altogether.

Customer: That's fine. Can I pay by card?

Shopkeeper: Yes, no problem at all. Here's your receipt.

Customer: Thank you very much.
