Key word help

  • Registration means signing up. You give your name and information to join.

    L'inscription.

  • A prescription is a note from a doctor. It says which medicine you need.

    L'ordonnance.

  • A walk-in clinic means you do not need an appointment. You can just go.

    La consultation sans rendez-vous.

  • A day ticket lets you travel on the bus all day for one price.

    Le billet à la journée.
