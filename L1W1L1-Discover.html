Introducing Yourself

Learn how to meet someone and share basic information.

Apprenez à rencontrer quelqu'un et à donner des informations simples.

Imagine you are joining an English class in your new city. You meet another learner for the first time.

  • I can say hello.
  • I can say my name.
  • I can say where I'm from.

Useful expressions

Seven expressions you can use today

One expression at a time. Listen, then move on when you are ready.

Une expression à la fois. Écoutez, puis continuez quand vous êtes prêt.

"Hello."

Use this when you meet someone.

Bonjour.

"My name is Amina."

Use this to say your name.

Je m'appelle Amina.

"What's your name?"

Use this to ask another person's name.

Comment vous appelez-vous ?

"I'm from France."

Use this to say your country.

Je viens de France.

"Where are you from?"

Use this to ask another person's country.

D'où venez-vous ?

"I live in Doha."

Use this to say your city.

J'habite à Doha.

"Nice to meet you."

Use this when meeting someone for the first time.

Enchanté(e).

Screen 6 of 9

Your turn

What would you say?

Three short questions. Try as many times as you like.

Trois questions courtes. Essayez autant de fois que vous voulez.

What would you say to tell someone your name?

Yes. "My name is..." tells someone your name.

What would you say to ask someone's country?

Exactly. "Where are you from?" asks about someone's country.

What would you say after meeting someone?

Good choice. "Nice to meet you." is useful English when you meet someone.
