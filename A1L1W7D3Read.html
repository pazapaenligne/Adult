    Read the text

    Read for the main message first.

    Lisez d'abord pour comprendre l'idée principale.

    pharmacist
    a person who gives out medicine and health advice at a pharmacy.
    appointment
    a fixed time to meet someone, like a pharmacist.
    prescription
    a paper or record that lets you collect medicine.
    medical card
    a card that shows your health details.
