Introducing Yourself

Learn how to meet a new person. Learn how to say your name.

Apprenez à rencontrer une nouvelle personne et à dire votre nom.

You are in a new English class. You meet a new person.

Vous êtes dans un nouveau cours d'anglais. Vous rencontrez une nouvelle personne.

  • I can say hello.
  • I can say my name.
  • I can say where I'm from.

Vous pouvez écouter chaque phrase autant de fois que vous voulez.

Screen 5 of 9

Useful expressions

Seven useful expressions

Listen to one expression. Then go to the next expression.

Écoutez une expression. Puis passez à la suivante.

"Hello."

Use this when you meet someone.

Bonjour.

"My name is Amina."

Use this to say your name.

Je m'appelle Amina.

"What's your name?"

Use this to ask a name.

Comment vous appelez-vous ?

"I'm from France."

Use this to say your country.

Je viens de France.

"Where are you from?"

Use this to ask a country.

D'où venez-vous ?

"I live in Doha."

Use this to say your city.

J'habite à Doha.

"Nice to meet you."

Use this when you meet a new person.

Enchanté(e).

Your turn

What would you say?

Three questions. You can try again and again.

Trois questions courtes. Essayez autant de fois que vous voulez.

You want to say your name. What do you say?

Yes. "My name is..." says your name.

You want to ask a country. What do you say?

Yes. "Where are you from?" asks about the country.

You meet a new person. What do you say?

Yes. You say "Nice to meet you." to a new person.
