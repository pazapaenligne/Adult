Making an appointment

Today's task: Practise useful English to request a routine appointment, choose a suitable time, and confirm the details.

Useful English for this situation

Look at these examples. One part of each sentence can change.

I'd like to make an appointment. Use this to ask politely for a visit.
Is the doctor available tomorrow? Ask if there is a free time.
Can I come in the afternoon? Change the time: morning or afternoon.
What documents do I need? Ask what to bring with you.
Could you repeat the address? Use this if you do not understand.
