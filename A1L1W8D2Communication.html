Your mission: ask for help at the school desk

WhoYou are a learner at a community school.
WhereAt the school reception desk.
PurposeYou have a problem with a form. You need help.
What you need to communicateExplain the problem, ask for help, and choose a solution.

Success goal: the receptionist understands your problem and knows how to help you. Perfect grammar is not needed. You can use invented details.

Choose the useful language
