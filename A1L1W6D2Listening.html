Listening Mission

Ordering at a restaurant

Listen once. What is the situation?

Waiter: Good evening. Table for two? Customer: Yes, please. Could we have a table near the window? Waiter: Of course. Here is the menu. Customer: Thank you. I would like the soup for my starter, and the chicken with rice for my main course. Waiter: Would you like a drink? Customer: Could I have a glass of water, please? Waiter: Of course. Customer: Thank you very much. Could we ask the waiter for the bill, please? Waiter: Of course. Here you are.

Listening Mission

Put the parts in order

Listen once more. Put the three parts of the visit in order.

Waiter: Good evening. Table for two? Customer: Yes, please. Could we have a table near the window? Waiter: Of course. Here is the menu. Customer: Thank you. I would like the soup for my starter, and the chicken with rice for my main course. Waiter: Would you like a drink? Customer: Could I have a glass of water, please? Waiter: Of course. Customer: Thank you very much. Could we ask the waiter for the bill, please? Waiter: Of course. Here you are.

  • 1 The customer asks for a table and looks at the menu.
  • 2 The customer orders food and a drink.
  • 3 The customer asks for the bill.
