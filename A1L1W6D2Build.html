Useful English for this situation

Today's task: Practise useful English for ordering at a restaurant — asking for a table, ordering food and drinks, and asking for the bill.

Look at these examples. One part of each sentence can change.

Could we have a table for two? Change the number of people.
Can I see the menu? Use this to ask politely.
I'd like the chicken, please. Change the food you want.
Could I have a glass of water? Change the drink you want.
Can we have the bill, please? Use this at the end of your meal.
