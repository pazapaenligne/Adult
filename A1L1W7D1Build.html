Build Useful English

The reception noticeboard

Today's task: You're helping at the health-centre reception desk. Learn the useful phrases pinned to the board before you start taking messages.

Read the notes on the board. One part of each sentence can change.

I have a headache. Change the word to say a different symptom: headache, cough, cold...
I feel tired. Change the feeling word.
My throat hurts. Change the body part.
I don't feel well. Use this when you feel ill in general.
I have had a fever since yesterday. Change the time word: since yesterday, since this morning...
