At the pharmacy or clinic

Today's task: Practise useful English for a pharmacy or clinic — describing a problem and understanding instructions.

Useful English for this situation

Look at these examples. One part of each sentence can change.

I need something for a headache. Change the word to say a different problem: headache, pain...
Where is the nearest pharmacy? Change the place: pharmacy, clinic...
How often should I take this? Ask about the amount or the time.
Could you repeat that, please? Use this to politely ask again.
I need to rest. Say what you need to do to feel better.
