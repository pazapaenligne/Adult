Week 15 · Lesson 1 · Invitations and arrangements

Build Useful English

Useful English for this situation.L’anglais utile pour cette situation.

Read each message. The underlined words can change.Lisez chaque message. Les mots soulignés peuvent changer.

  1. Invite someoneInviter quelqu’un
    Would you like to join us?
    Change it: Would you like to visit tomorrow?
  2. Ask about timeDemander si on est libre
    Are you free on Saturday?
    Change it: Are you free on Sunday?
  3. Say yesDire oui
    Thank you for the invitation. I’d love to come.
  4. Say no politelyRefuser poliment
    Sorry, I’m not available.
  5. Suggest another timeProposer un autre moment
    Could we meet another time?
    Change it: Could we meet tomorrow?

Dotted underline = you can change these words.Soulignement en pointillés = vous pouvez changer ces mots.
