💬 Communication Mission

Meet a new classmate.

You are at your English class. You meet someone for the first time.

Introduce yourself and find out where the other person is from.

Your mission

  • Say hello.
  • Say your name.
  • Ask their name.
  • Say where you are from.
  • Ask where they are from.
  • Say “Nice to meet you.”

Remember

  • Hello.
  • My name is…
  • What's your name?
  • I'm from…
  • Where are you from?
  • Nice to meet you.

You can look at these expressions if you need help.

You do not need the exact words. “Hi, I'm Amina.” is as good as “Hello. My name is Amina.” Your message is what matters.
