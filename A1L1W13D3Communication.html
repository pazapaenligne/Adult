Asking for clarification

Your mission

An online activity is not clear. Ask the course support team for help.

WhoYou, a learner on an online English course
WhereThe course chat with the support team
PurposeUnderstand an unclear activity and ask for help
What you need to communicateWhat you don't understand, your request for help, and where to submit your answer
Success goal: the support team understands your problem and knows how to help you.

You can use invented details.
