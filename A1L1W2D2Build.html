    Useful English

    1. Full hours: o'clock

    Use o'clock for a full hour.
    It's nine o'clock.

    2. Half hours: half past

    Half past means :30.
    Half past two.

    3. Useful schedule words

    start
    when it begins
    finish
    when it ends
    open
    ready for customers
    close
    not open
    late
    after the correct time

    Speaking

    1. Prepare your schedule card

    Optional practice — this activity does not affect lesson completion.

    2. Ask and answer with a partner

    A: What time does the class start?

    B: It starts at six o'clock.

    A: What time does it finish?

    B: It finishes at seven.

    A: What time is the appointment?

    B: It is at half past two.

    Change roles. Use different times from your schedule card.

    3. Real-life mini-scenarios

    Class — Ask when it starts and finishes.
    Appointment — Check the time and ask if it is morning or afternoon.
    Shop — Ask when it opens and closes.
    Working alone? Choose two scenarios and write both sides of each conversation.
