Pazapa English Programme for Adults Communication Mission

Your mission

Week 5 · Lesson 2 · Prices and quantities

Who
Your neighbour, Hana, is going to Pazapa Market this afternoon. Votre voisine, Hana, va au marché Pazapa cet après-midi.
Where
You are at home. You will send Hana a short text message. Vous êtes chez vous. Vous allez envoyer un court message à Hana.
Purpose
Ask Hana to buy some items for you and check the price. Demandez à Hana d'acheter des articles pour vous et vérifiez le prix.
What to say
Which items, how many, and how much you want to pay. Quels articles, combien, et combien vous voulez payer.
Success: Hana understands what to buy and your price limit. Réussite : Hana comprend quoi acheter et votre limite de prix.

You can invent any details you like. You never need to use your real name, address or money information.

Choose the useful language

Choose 3 or 4 phrases that will help you write your message to Hana. Not every phrase is useful for this mission. Choisissez 3 ou 4 phrases utiles pour écrire votre message à Hana. Toutes les phrases ne sont pas utiles pour cette mission.

Show example

Can I have one kilo of rice, please? Also one bottle of water. Can you check the price? Under 15 riyals, please.

Thank you so much! See you later.

This is only a model. It will not change your own plan.
