Plan the messagePlanifiez le message

Put phrases into three parts: opening, key information, and closing. You can change your plan. Placez des expressions dans trois parties : ouverture, information clé et conclusion. Vous pouvez modifier votre plan.

Example planExemple de plan

Opening: Hello, Mrs Hale.

Key information: I'm sorry, I can't come tomorrow. I'm available on Monday morning. I can work from nine to two.

Closing: Could we change the meeting time? Thank you.
