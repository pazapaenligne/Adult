Utilisez les informations pour faire un choix.

Lina wants to buy a shirt with a discount. She also wants to try it on quickly, without waiting in a queue. Which day should she go?

Tuesday has 20% off shirts, and Saturday is the busy day — so Tuesday lets Lina get the discount and try the shirt on quickly.
